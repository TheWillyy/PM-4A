# Reyda — 15s showreel storyboard

120 BPM · 1 beat = 0.5 s · 30 beats (b0–b29) · 1920×1080 @ 60 fps

**Through-line object: the Qualify check.** The violet check chip from the product's
Qualify button (the only violet control in the real UI). Every scene unfolds out of it
and folds back into it. It ends as the logo tile. The accent violet is used only on the
chip and on one key word per caption.

Sources: the four supplied UI SVGs (Find · Qualify before reading · Drafts from past bids ·
Know the moment a tender changes). All UI copy and numbers are copied from them.

Camera: it never stops. A cumulative 4% push per scene (×1.04 per scene, continuous across
cuts), plus screen-studio focus moves on critically damped springs.

Audio (Web Audio, OfflineAudioContext): a kick on every beat; offbeat hats; an Am–F–C–G bass;
a stab and noise hit on each scene change (b2, b4, b10, b16, b26), the biggest at b20;
ticks on cursor clicks.
