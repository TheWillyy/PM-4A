# Reyda: "Meet Emma", 62s product demo (v3)

1920×1080 · 30 fps · no voiceover · 120 BPM (1 beat = 0.5 s, 1 bar = 2 s)
Story: **find → understand the buyer → prepare → hand back to the team.** Emma prepares; the team reviews and submits.
One IT tender carries the story: *IT Infrastructure Modernisation & Cloud Migration*, Stadt Musterstadt (fictional), bid by Nordwerk IT GmbH (fictional).
Brand: violet `#7A59E3`, ink `#17112F`/`#2E2A45`, lavender `#F3F1F9`; Wittgenstein semi-bold headlines, Inter UI.
Assets: logo pill from `demo assets/Frame 320.svg`; Emma mascot from `demo assets/emma mascot.svg`;
tender forms TW 01 (page 1) and TW 02 (pages 3 and 8) rendered from the supplied .docx files, with only the
buyer's address on TW 01 replaced by the fictional buyer. Emma's entries are a layer on top of the real pages.
Music: `assets/music.mp3`, generated by `tools/music.cjs`; every cue below is a hit in that track.
Each scene's small grey chore line names the work it replaces. There are no time-saving claims.
